What this site keeps on your device

No cookies, no trackers, no requests to other companies. Two entries in your browser's local storage, set by the site itself:

  • pm-basket: your basket, delivery or pickup and postcode, so a reload doesn't lose your order. Kept for 14 days, then ignored.
  • pm-intro: the date the opening animation last played, so it shows once every 30 days.

For the owner: check with your data-protection adviser whether pm-intro is covered by the TDDDG §25 exemption.
